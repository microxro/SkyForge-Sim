# App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect)

SKYFORGE is a free, open-source flight simulator. No sign-in is needed and there are no accounts.

To test: launch the app, tap "Fly now: Monterey (real world, works offline)". Flight data for six airports is bundled, so no network is needed. "Choose an airport" lists them; looking up other airports downloads small public map tiles (hosts listed in docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) (5 to 15 MB) once.

Items marked "not yet available" (world map, settings) are intentionally disclosed placeholders and are not reachable features.

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, tracking or in-app purchases. Data attributions: Menu > About > Acknowledgements and Data Attribution. Source: https://github.com/microxro/SkyForge-Sim
