# SKYFORGE privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

SKYFORGE does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

## What stays on your device
Settings and cached map tiles are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

## Network requests
When you fly somewhere that is not bundled, or request weather, the app downloads public data directly from these providers: AWS Open Data terrain tiles, USGS The National Map, EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless tiles, and NOAA Aviation Weather Center. Those requests carry only the map tile or station identifier and, as with any web request, your IP address, which is visible to the provider. We do not receive or log these requests. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

## Motion sensors
On iPhone and iPad the app may read device tilt to steer the aircraft. This data is used on the device only and is never stored or sent.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
