## Description
SKYFORGE is a free, open-source flight simulator for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Fly a light aircraft from real-world airports built from open data: satellite and aerial imagery, elevation, OpenStreetMap buildings and roads, and land cover. A procedural "SkyWorld" airfield is included too.

- Real airports included in the app and flyable offline
- Physically based sky with day-to-twilight lighting
- Custom Metal renderer with terrain, buildings, roads and cockpit
- Steam gauges and a glass-cockpit primary flight display
- Touch, tilt, keyboard and game controller input, with rebindable controls
- Any-airport lookup that downloads small public imagery and elevation tiles once and caches them
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking

SKYFORGE is open source under the Apache License 2.0. Data attributions are shown in the app under "Acknowledgements and Data Attribution".

Some planned features (missions, a world map, settings) are not available yet and are labelled as such in the app.
