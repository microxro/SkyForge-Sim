# SF-1 vs JSBSim c172p oracle comparison

Generated by `tools/jsbsim_oracle/compare.py`. Offline development check only: JSBSim and its c172p data (LGPL code; aircraft data under its own MIT/LGPL-style licences) are never shipped in the app and nothing was copied from them into the Swift sources.

* Oracle: JSBSim 1.3.3, `c172p`, loaded to 1000.0 kg (copilot + passenger point masses; CG 43.4 in).
* Subject: `sf1_mirror.py` (Python mirror of `SFFlightModel`; Swift emitter output can be dropped in as `--sf1`, see SWIFT_EMITTER.md).
* The mirror matches `SFFlightModel` at git HEAD when written (FlightDynamics.swift 389 lines); later working-tree changes (Mach schedule, engine model, stall behaviour) are not ported, so re-run after re-porting.
* Identical scenarios and control laws on both sides (`scenarios.py`); both run at 120 Hz, ISA, no wind, sea-level runway.
* Result: **11 PASS, 10 WARN, 2 FAIL** (3 informational, 0 missing).
* The c172p is a different aircraft with different real-world numbers; bands are sanity bands, not a certification of equality.

## Biggest discrepancies and likely causes

1. **Sideslip per deg rudder (wings level)** (FAIL): SF-1 -1.52 vs JSBSim -0.926 deg/deg (+63.8%). SF-1 cnRudder=-0.0657 vs c172p -0.043 (+53%) with similar cnBeta (0.065 vs 0.059). [what-if] cnRudder=-0.043 recovers the c172p value.
2. **Trim shaft power** (FAIL): SF-1 79.1 vs JSBSim 63.5 kW (+24.7%). SF-1 cD0=0.031 vs c172p 0.027 (+15%). [what-if] cD0=0.027 recovers part of the gap; the remainder is likely the flat prop efficiency (eta 0.80 in the thrust model) and the induced-drag factor (oswald 0.75).
3. **Steady roll rate per deg aileron** (WARN): SF-1 1.89 vs JSBSim 3.35 deg/s/deg (-43.6%). Same cause as the roll-rate row (adverse yaw / clBeta), plus clAileron 0.178 vs 0.229.
4. **CLmax full flaps** (WARN): SF-1 1.89 vs JSBSim 1.61  (+17.4%). cLMaxFlaps=2.05 declared (about 1.89 measured as total CL) vs c172p about 1.6; the SF-1 flap lift increment is cLFlap=0.55 vs the c172p table's 0.35 at 30 deg.
5. **Steady roll rate, 50% stick** (WARN): SF-1 17.0 vs JSBSim 25.1 deg/s (-32.3%). SF-1 cnAileron=-0.053 is ~10x the c172p value (-0.0053): strong adverse yaw builds sideslip whose dihedral effect (clBeta) throttles the roll. Likely a decimal slip. [what-if] cnAileron=-0.0053 restores the c172p roll rate.
6. **Trim elevator (TE up +)** (WARN): SF-1 -0.15 vs JSBSim -3.14 deg (+3.00 deg). SF-1 cm0=0.02, cmAlpha=-0.89 vs c172p cm0=0.10, cmAlpha=-1.8. [what-if] cm0/cmAlpha at c172p values moves the trim elevator to the c172p value.
7. **Alpha at CLmax full flaps** (WARN): SF-1 13.1 vs JSBSim 16.0 deg (-2.93 deg). Same mechanism as the clean case: the SF-1 peak sits at (cLMaxFlaps - cL0 - cLFlap) / cLAlpha = 13.3 deg, the c172p at about 16 deg.
8. **Short-period natural frequency** (WARN): SF-1 5.02 vs JSBSim 6.48 rad/s (-22.6%). Static longitudinal stability: cmAlpha=-0.89 vs c172p -1.8 (about half, so wn ~ sqrt(2) lower). [what-if] cmAlpha=-1.8 gives c172p's wn.

Other WARN/FAIL rows: Distance to 55 ft (+18.9%); Ground roll to liftoff (+18.3%); Alpha at CLmax clean (-2.24 deg); Liftoff speed (+8.2%).

### Unused or inconsistent parameters spotted while porting

* `AeroCoefficients.cmAlphaDot` is never read by `forcesAndMoments` (no alpha-dot damping term); `cLQ` is applied but there is no `cLAlphaDot`.
* `AircraftConfig.stallAlpha` and `ixz` are declared but unused by the dynamics; the effective stall is set by `cLMaxClean/cLMaxFlaps` and the fixed 2.5 deg break offset.
* Measured CLmax (about 1.42 clean / 1.89 flaps) is below the declared 1.55 / 2.05 mainly because the full-aft elevator subtracts tail lift (cLElevator*de, about -0.19) from the total CL, plus softplus rounding (w = 0.06) before the sigmoid break.
* Post-stall: the SF-1 loses lift abruptly (CL retained 4 s after the peak is low) while the c172p mushes at near-constant CL. That is a design choice, not necessarily a bug.

## Method notes and caveats

* Stall: power off, wings held level, pitch attitude ramped at 1 deg/s through a pitch-hold loop that saturates at full aft stick; speed is read at the CL peak. The achieved deceleration differs between aircraft (reported, informational) because idle drag and flight-path energy differ; stall speed is insensitive to it (checked at 0.35-1.4 deg/s).
* CL is wind-axis lift / (qbar S) on both sides, i.e. total aerodynamic CL including tail and pitch-rate terms.
* Short-period and phugoid values come from least-squares damped-sinusoid fits (`scenarios.fit_damped`); the short period of both aircraft is well damped so the fit conditions (wn, zeta) are less precise than the phugoid.
* Takeoff: same rotation law (55 KCAS, 9 deg pitch target), heading-hold on rudder/nose-wheel, height gain 55 ft measured at the CG relative to its static height. Runway friction, gear geometry and brake models differ.
* c172p control travels differ (elevator 28/23 deg, ailerons -20/+15, rudder 16 deg vs SF-1 25/18/20 deg); dynamics metrics use deflection in degrees where possible, step metrics use 50% stick.
* JSBSim's longitudinal trim leaves about 1 fpm residual climb; engine/propeller states are not reset after trim.
